Choose your password

The admin made you an organizer account. Choose a password (at least 10 characters) to finish; you then sign in with your login and this password.

Start again from zero — for tests, or a new season. The super admin (the master key) only. Nothing is thrown away: what you take away moves to a dated folder on the server (reset-<date> in the Arena’s data folder) and can be fetched back by hand. Accounts, the install key and the settings never move.

Take away

What you keep stays whole: a tournament you keep keeps the Table check its result came from, and a tournament or a cup you keep keeps its image.

My account

LAN only. Create the tournament and add the players. Then either let the director open the table on the LAN server (invitations, seat check, hand-over to the first player, result recorded by itself), or set the result yourself — typed in, taken from a Table check, or from a PokerTH log. Every action goes into the tournament’s sealed journal; the validated result is signed.

Table settings

Used when the director opens the table: the same choices as the web client's « Create a table » form.

The players are drawn into tables of at most 10, played at the same time; the first of each table play the final table, which gives the ranking.

Tournament

Pick a tournament, or create one.

Pick a server of the LAN directory (or the local server), choose a table, and a director watches it as a spectator until the end: every event goes into a sealed journal and the ranking is signed. Nothing is created, kicked or posted on the game server.

    Pick a watch, or start one.

    A cup gathers tournaments. Points: each counted tournament gives every player the number of players finished behind him, plus one (a shared place: the average); the standings add up each player's best results. Stages: the first of a tournament go on to the next one, up to a final — which may also take the top of the points standings. Only validated results count; closing the cup signs its standings.
