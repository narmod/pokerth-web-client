    LAN only. Create the tournament and add the players.       3 · Watch

      Pick a watch, or start one.

      A cup gathers tournaments. Points: each counted tournament gives every player the number of players finished behind him, plus one (a shared place: the average); the standings add up each player's best results. Stages: the first of a tournament go on to the next one, up to a final — which may also take the top of the points standings. Only validated results count; closing the cup signs its standings. Programme: say how the cup runs (a series, a series and a final, steps, one tournament), its dates and how players register — the cup makes its tournaments, the director opens them, and the cup qualifies and closes by itself.
